The Growth in Cases

NCMEC released an average of reported sexual child images over two decades.

1998: Over 3,000 sexual child images were reported
2000: 100,000 reports
2014: One million reports
2018: Over 45 million videos and images were flagged

More data presented by the NCMEC following the first six months of 2025 and the reports sent into their site. Comparing the number of reports from January to June of 2024 and 2025, show a horrible growth in cases.

Jan-June 2024: 292,951 reports
Jan-June 2025: 518,720 reports

225,769 Increase

More specifically, focusing on financial sextortion cases, in 2025 along there were approximately 50,000 reports, which is about 137 tips a day.

The Perpetrators

As listed in The Victims section, the same 2015 study with 5,863 reports, there were 5,917 offenders.

87% of them were men
5% were women
9% unknown

How do offenders manipulate children online?

  • Engage the child in sexual conversation or roleplay
  • Ask the child for explicit content
  • Develop a positive rapport (compliments, praise, acting caring and empathic)
  • Sending/offering the child explicit sexual images of themselves
  • Pretending to be younger
  • Asking children to exchange pictures
  • Offering other things for pictures (money, promises, substances, etc)

NCMEC curated an approximate percentage of what offender's goals are when they interact with minors and explicit content of children.

  • 68.3% - Sexual Content
  • 32% - Sex
  • 7.6% - Explicit Conversations
  • 1.56% - Financial Sextortion
  • ?% - Other

Other includes trafficking, messages to children, intent to distribute children's content, exhibitionist goals, and more.

Around the World

Shocking data from Childlight, discloses that around 12% of the world's children have been victims of non-consensual taking or sharing of sexual images. This is approximately over 302 million children.

The USA is one of the leading countries in child exploitation. Around 14 million American men have admitted to sexually offending a child online before.

The Middle East and North Africa have the most CSAM alerts (content being hosted or uploaded in these countries).

The USA and Western Europe follows in 2nd and 3rd highest CSAM Rate. The Netherlands and the U.S. has the largest number of CSAM reports sent to an individual country.

The Victims

NCMEC reports out of 5,863 reports in 2015:

4% were young boys
8% older boys
24% younger girls
48% older girls

The CDC and the National Children's Alliance confirmed that from reports in the USA the CSA victims are generally 1/4 girls and 1/20 boys.

NCMEC goes onto provide that approximately 1/5 children in Europe are victims of CSA and over 1 billion children globally.

Children who are more susceptible to grooming from online predators are due to prior vulnerabilities. Which can include mental health issues, abuse, trading sexual pics previously, prior experience with talking to adults online, etc explains NCMEC.
