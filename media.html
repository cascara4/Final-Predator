The Show that Started Everything

The TV Series: To Catch a Predator, aired on Dateline NBC from 2004 to 2007. A digital advocacy group, Perverted Justice, posed as underage children in chat rooms to catch adults who showed up to "sting houses." Where they would get confronted by journalist Chris Hansen.

"Why don't you take a seat right over there?"

TCAP grew in popularity due to it using the fear on stranger danger, vigilantism, and the interest in humiliation and punishment.

However, the show was put to an end in December 2007 due to a sting ending in the suicide of Dr Louis William "Bill" Conradt Jr.

Film

Primetime (2026) is a psychological thriller that stems around the tv series To Catch a Predator. Following the last sting before the show ended and the twisted side of journalists and greed. Robert Pattinson portrays an exaggerated character of Chris Hansen.

The 2025 documentary Predators, directed by David Osit, examines the rise and fall of the TCAP show and Chris Hansen himself. The documentary focuses on the line between investigative journalism and entertainment, public humiliation and the affects of sting operations.

A high rating at both the 2025 documentary and the 2026 film brought more attention to the TCAP show while also addressing the crisis of child extortion worldwide.

Online Pred-Catchers

Predator Catchers are usually decentralized vigilante groups whose goal is to hunt down online predators. Positive results have come from these groups, they operate with more speed, adaptability and willingness than authorities. However there is a lot of grey area with ethics, some members of theses groups use violence and excessive force - which complicates the legal process

Content Creators

Content Creator Predator Catcher groups have received backlash from media and law enforcement. Weather the team is doing these stings for the money over the mission to protect children.

Due to change in municipal law, some of these 3rd party stings can't be followed through in the justice system. Some predator catchers act out, physically assaulting the adults that they've lured to a sting.
