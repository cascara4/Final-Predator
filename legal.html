Legal Actions

The rise of online child extortion forced the U.S. government to respond with social campaigns, laws and bills. I'm only listing some of the most recent policy changes.

Know2Protect is a national public awareness campaign to combat online Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSEA). It aims to educate children, parents, trusted adults and policymakers to prevent CSEA. This campaign is used to help individuals learn how to report online enticement and what characteristics to look out for.

Learn more about Know2Protect.

Protect Our Children Reauthorization Act of 2025

This bill extends federal funding to $240 million for the Department of Justice's Internet Against Children (ICAC) Task Force program. While also requiring the taskforce to increase the investigative capacity of law enforcement in order to identify child victims and report said victims.

It also required the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) to provide more information to law enforcement agencies when referring an online report.

Read more about the PROTECT Our Children Reauthorization Act of 2025.

Renewed Hope Act (June 2026)

This renewed bill provided over $108.5 million to Homeland Security Investigation (HSI) for 200 more personnel.

  • 40 forensic analysts
  • 30 child exploitation investigators
  • 130 analysts and investigators at offices of the Special Agents in Charge

Before the Department of Homeland Security only had 7 full time specialists in an effort against CSAM.
The increasement of employees hopes to address the child abuse imagery and fight against ai-driven tactics.
