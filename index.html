HOW THE INTERNET HAS CHANGED CHILD EXPLOITATION - A RESEARCH SITE

Have you seen the rise of child predators in the news? New cases of child exploitation across the country and even the world. Important public figures exposed for participating in child extortion rings.

Have these offenders been around forever?
Or is the fast communication of the internet the cause?

What is this site?

This is a research-based site that delves into how the internet changed child exploitation, for the better or worse.

Chief Executive Paul Stanfield, previously an active officer for the National Crime Agency and Interpol, spoke on the rise of child exploitation.

"It's a global pandemic, a global health emergency and we need to look at it the way we considered Aids in the 1980s, and in the same way we treated Covid."
